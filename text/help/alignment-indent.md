## To align a paragraph

Place the caret in the paragraph (or heading) you want to align — no need to select all the text. Then either:

- Click one of the four alignment buttons in the toolbar — left, center, right, justify.
- Open **Format → Align & indent** and pick an entry.
- Use a keyboard shortcut:
  - **⌘⇧L** — Align left
  - **⌘⇧E** — Align center
  - **⌘⇧R** — Align right
  - **⌘⇧J** — Justify

Alignment applies to paragraphs and headings. List items and blockquotes are excluded — their alignment comes from the surrounding block.

## To indent a paragraph

With the caret in the target paragraph:

- Click the **Increase indent** or **Decrease indent** button in the toolbar.
- Use **⌘]** to increase, **⌘[** to decrease.
- Or open **Format → Align & indent → Increase / Decrease indent**.

Each level adds 36 px (matching Word's standard half-inch indent). Indents go from 0 to 8 levels deep — the toolbar buttons disable at the bounds. Indent depth round-trips through `.docx` so docs imported from Word keep their layout.
