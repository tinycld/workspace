## What the Directory is

The **Directory** view (sidebar → Directory) shows everyone in your organization — not your personal contacts. It's the answer to "who's in this org with me, and what's their role?"

It does not pull from your contact list. Members appear here because they have a `user_org` row linking them to this org, regardless of whether you have them in Contacts.

## What each row shows

- Avatar (initials, with a color derived from the name).
- Name (first + last).
- Email address.
- A colored **role badge**: Owner, Admin, Member, or Guest.

Role colors:

- **Owner** — primary color
- **Admin** — info color (typically blue)
- **Member** — success color (typically green)
- **Guest** — warning color (typically amber)

## Searching the directory

The search box at the top filters by name or email — substring match, no prefix needed.

## Adding directory members to your contacts

The Directory is read-only — it doesn't include a "Save to Contacts" action. To add an org member to your personal address book, copy their email or name and use **+ Create contact**.

## Privacy

Anyone in the org can see the Directory. Roles are public within the org. If you want to limit visibility of who's in an org, that's a question for [Organizations](help://core:organizations).
