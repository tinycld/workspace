## Jumping to Contacts

From anywhere in the app, press **t** then **o** to jump to Contacts. (The single-key shortcut **o** also works once you're inside the package.)

## In the Contacts list

| Shortcut | Action |
|---|---|
| **j** | Move focus to the next row |
| **k** | Move focus to the previous row |
| **Enter** | Open the focused contact |
| **c** | Create a new contact |

The focus indicator only appears after you've used `j` or `k` — until then, rows render without a focus ring. This avoids a stray highlight when you arrive from a click.

## Search

The search box is just a regular text input — click into it (or tab in) and type. There's no dedicated shortcut to focus it. **Esc** clears the search.
