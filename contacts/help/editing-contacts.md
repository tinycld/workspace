## Opening a contact

Click any row in the Contacts list. You can also press **j** / **k** to move between rows and **Enter** to open the focused one.

## What you can edit

The detail screen exposes every field of the contact: first name, last name, company, job title, email, phone, notes, and the favorite toggle.

You also see the contact's **labels** above the form — click the labels area to add or remove labels. See [Labels](help://contacts:labels).

## Saving

Edits commit when you click **Save**. The contact updates immediately for you and propagates to other sessions (and CardDAV clients) within seconds via real-time updates.

If a field validates (e.g. an invalid email format), the form highlights it before saving and the **Save** button stays disabled.

## Returning to the list

The back arrow at the top-left returns to wherever you came from — the main list, Favorites, a label view, or the Deleted bin.

## Deleting from the detail screen

There is no delete button on the detail screen — return to the list and use the row's action menu. See [Favorites, deletion, and restore](help://contacts:favorites-and-deletion).
